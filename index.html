    Social Courts

    All skill levels are welcome. When two or three paddles are waiting, one pair rotates off and the next two go on. When four or more paddles are waiting, all four players rotate off and the next four go on.

    Challenge Court

    Always two on, two off when a pair is waiting. Winners stay and the next two paddles go on, even when four or more are waiting.

    All Courts

    Put one paddle per player at the back of the queue for the court you want. Play doubles to 11, win by 2. Clear the court promptly after your game.

    Pickleball Rules

    Scoring

    Play doubles to 11, win by 2. Only the serving team scores. Before serving, call your team’s score first, the other team’s score second, and your server number last.

    Serving

    Serve diagonally from behind the baseline, past the kitchen line. Use an underhand volley serve or a drop serve. Only one player serves on the opening turn. After that, both partners get a turn before the serve goes to the other team.

    Two-bounce rule

    The serve must bounce on the receiving side, and the return must bounce on the serving side. After those two bounces, either team may volley.

    The kitchen

    You can step into the kitchen to hit a ball after it bounces. It’s a fault to touch the kitchen or its line during a volley, including when your momentum carries you in afterward.

    Line calls

    A ball touching a line is in, except a serve that touches the kitchen line. Call a ball out only when you clearly see it land outside the line.
